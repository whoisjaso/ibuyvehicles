# iBuy Vehicles: Image Brief (Night Tow Livery)

The redesign is drawn in code: the truck door, the beacon, the chevron tape and the lettering are all built into the page, so it already looks finished without any images. Photos now do one job, which is proving Mark is real. That means most of what's below is **real photography**. ChatGPT is only used for the share image and an optional night backdrop.

**Status:** 0 of 7 done.

## The two rules

1. **Nothing generated may pose as Mark, his lot, his customers, or a car he bought.** The "Real Cars Mark Bought" strip and the Mark portrait are real phone photos only.
2. **No text, logos, badges or license plates** in any generated image.

---

## Real photos (an iPhone and ten minutes)

Shoot on the main camera at 1x, at golden hour or just after sunset. The site is a night world, so dusk light with headlights or a tow truck's amber beacon on looks best.

| # | Where it goes | What to shoot |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | "Mark Answers His Own Phone." (portrait 4:5) | **Mark**, waist up, standing at the cab door of his tow truck or beside a car he just bought, looking at the camera with a calm half-smile. Dusk, with the truck's lights on behind him. Use Portrait mode. This is the most valuable image on the site. |
| **R2** | Real Cars strip (portrait 4:5) | A car on the flatbed bed, winch hooked, shot low from the rear corner at dusk with the beacon on. |
| **R3** | Real Cars strip (landscape 4:3) | A customer's car in the driveway before pickup. Ask the owner first, and keep house numbers out of frame. |
| **R4** | Real Cars strip (portrait 4:5) | Hands only: cash being handed over beside an open car door. No faces. |
| **R5** | Real Cars strip (landscape 4:3) | The truck pulling up to a pickup, headlights on. |
| **R6** | Footer and Google listing | The front of 8150 W Little York Rd from across the street, so people know they're in the right place. |

Send them in any size. I'll crop them, grade them into the night palette, and swap them in. The four current photos are low resolution and came from the old site.
