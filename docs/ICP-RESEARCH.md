# iBuy Vehicles: Who The Seller Is, What Hurts, What To Say

Research for the site, the estimator, and Mark's phone calls. Compiled October 2026 from public reviews, forums, news and Texas law. Every quote below is real and linked. Items marked **[snippet]** came from a search summary and were not read on the source page; items marked **[verify]** need Mark or counsel before they go on the site.

## How this was researched, and the gaps

- **Read in full:** Trustpilot pages for Peddle, Wheelzy, Junk My Car, Clunker Junker, CashForCars and Carvana; PissedConsumer; car and caregiver forums (AnandTech, Quarter to Three, Sawmill Creek, ForEbodiesOnly, ForABodiesOnly, Caregivers.com); Texas TV news (KPRC Houston, Fox 7 Austin, NBC DFW); TxDMV pages and Texas statutes; competitor websites.
- **Blocked from this environment:** Reddit (refused to the research tools), YouTube comments, Yelp, BobIsTheOilGuy, Nextdoor and Houston Facebook groups. **No Reddit or YouTube material is in this document.** A 30-minute manual pass is worth doing in a normal browser: r/askcarsales, r/povertyfinance, r/houston, r/legaladvice with "junk car", "Peddle", "no title Texas", "tow driver lowered offer", plus the comments under "Peddle review" and "Wheelzy review" YouTube videos.
- **Thin:** first-hand dealer accounts (aged inventory), first-hand Houston flood-car sellers, and Houston-specific junk-buyer reviews. Mark's own Google reviews and his competitors' Google reviews are the fastest way to fill this.
- Review sites are self-reported. Most competitor reviews are positive; the complaints below are a minority, but they are consistent and specific, and they are exactly what a nervous seller fears.

| Segment | Their situation | What they need to hear first |

## 5. What makes them finally sell (triggers)

1. The car has to leave the driveway or the lot. ("Needed to get a car out of the driveway.")
2. It broke down at the roadside. ("transmission out… broken down on the side of the road.")
3. The repair quote is close to, or more than, what the car is worth.
4. A death in the family or an inheritance.
5. They need money today. ("when I needed the money that day.")
6. A flood total. (Harvey context only: Cox Automotive estimated 300,000 to 500,000 Houston-area vehicles lost. [Cox](https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/hurricane-harvey-the-worst-disaster-for-automobiles-ever/))
7. City pressure: Houston places red placards on junked vehicles, which starts a 10-day abatement clock. ([City of Houston](https://pwehelp.houstontx.gov/council/committees/rna/20220712/Junk-Motor-Vehicles.pdf))
8. **Not found first-hand:** HOA notices, apartment tow warnings, failed inspections. Likely real, but unproven.

## 6. Texas facts that turn fear into a checklist

| Situation | The real path | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Lost title | Certified copy (VTR-34): $2 by mail or $5.45 in person, photo ID, original lien release if there was a loan. | [TxDMV](https://www.txdmv.gov/motorists/buying-or-selling-a-vehicle/get-a-copy-of-your-title) |
| Owner died, no probate | Affidavit of heirship (VTR-262), all signatures notarized. | [TxDMV VTR-262](https://www.txdmv.gov/sites/default/files/form_files/VTR-262.pdf) |
| No title at all | Bonded title, a tax-office hearing, or a court order. Not available for junked or nonrepairable vehicles. | [TxDMV](https://www.txdmv.gov/motorists/buying-or-selling-a-vehicle/bought-a-vehicle-with-no-title) |
| **Old car, no title (new law)** | Since Sept 1, 2025 (HB 5436), a licensed used auto parts recycler may buy a vehicle 13+ years old, unregistered 7+ years, for parts or scrap only, with the seller's photo ID and a signed statement; it must be reported to TxDMV. **[verify: only if iBuy holds the right licence]** | [HB 5436](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/html/HB05436F.htm) |
| After the sale | File the Vehicle Transfer Notification (VTR-346) within 30 days, and the seller is protected from the buyer's tolls and tickets (Transp. Code §501.147). Take the plates and toll tag off. | [TxDMV](https://www.txdmv.gov/motorists/buying-or-selling-a-vehicle), [§501.147](https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._transp._code_section_501.147) |
| Flood | Insurer-totaled flood cars carry a "Flood Damage" title remark. **[verify: uninsured flooded cars]** | [TxDMV manual](https://www.txdmv.gov/sites/default/files/body-files/Salvage_Nonrepairable_Manual.pdf) |
| Cash payment | No Texas ban found on paying cash for a whole vehicle. **[verify with counsel]** | [Occ. Code §1956](https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._occ._code_section_1956.001) |

## 8. Probing questions (estimator, text and phone)

Use these to qualify the seller and make the right path obvious. Each one maps to a fear above.

1. **"What's going on with the car?"** (runs / starts but won't drive / won't start). This is the problem, in their words.
2. **"Is anyone waiting on this money?"** (today / this week / no rush). This shows urgency, so Mark can prioritise without asking "how desperate are you".
3. **"Whose name is on the title?"** (mine / someone else's / a family member who passed / no title / not sure). This is the title route.
4. **"Is there still a loan on it?"** This catches lien problems before pickup, not at the curb.
5. **"Has anything been replaced or removed?"** (catalytic converter, engine, battery, wheels). This kills the category's number-one excuse up front: anything that changes the price is named before the number, not after.
6. **"Have you been given another number?"** (amount, from whom). This sets up the second opinion.
7. **"Where is it sitting, and can a truck get to it?"** (driveway, street, apartment lot, garage, flat tyres). This decides the pickup plan.
8. **"Do you have the keys?"**
9. **"When could you be there?"** (or "do you need to be there?"). This decides the pickup window.
