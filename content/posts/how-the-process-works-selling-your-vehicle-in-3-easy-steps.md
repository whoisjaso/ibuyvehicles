At **I Buy Vehicles**, we’ve simplified the selling process into **three easy steps** so you don’t waste time with complicated procedures. Here’s how it works:

## Step 1: Tell Us About Your Vehicle

Provide a few basic details about your vehicle, such as:

- Make & model
- Year of manufacture
- Mileage
- Current condition

This helps us evaluate your vehicle and give you a fair price.

## Step 2: Get Your Offer

Once we have the details, we’ll give you a starting estimate. Mark then makes a **no-obligation offer** himself after a quick look at the vehicle, based on current market values. He asks about anything that changes the price (like a missing catalytic converter) before he gives you a number, not at the curb. You never have to sell.

## Step 3: Sell & Get Paid

If you’re happy with the offer, we’ll complete the paperwork and you are paid on the spot, before the car is towed. Towing is free anywhere in Houston, with no hidden fees. Just a straightforward, stress-free sale.

**Tip:** We accept all vehicles, running or not. Even if your car is old, damaged, or has high mileage, we’ll still make you a fair offer.

With **I Buy Vehicles**, selling your car, bike, or truck has never been easier.
