## Paperwork Help for a More Straightforward Transaction

Selling a car isn’t just a matter of agreeing on a price. Ownership documents and transfer requirements matter as well. Sellers should know which documents are needed and make sure the transaction is properly recorded. Things can get more complicated if the title has been misplaced or other paperwork is incomplete.

iBuyVehicles helps with the documents related to the sale and explains what to do next. If a **title is lost**, tell the team before pickup and check which route fits your car. If the car is titled to you, a certified copy of the title from TxDMV is usually the simplest fix. Older junk cars that have not been registered for years may qualify for the Texas no-title route through a licensed recycler.

Documentation can differ by transaction and vehicle, so check it before the sale. After the sale, file a Vehicle Transfer Notification with TxDMV within 30 days. This support is another reason some sellers prefer working with an established vehicle buyer rather than managing every detail on their own.
