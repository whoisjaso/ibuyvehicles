## What Determines the Cash Offer for Your Vehicle?

No single price applies to all used or junk cars. Several details matter in a **vehicle appraisal in Houston**, and a vehicle’s age is only one of them.

Factors that may influence an offer are:

- **Make, model, and year:** Demand and the availability of usable parts vary by vehicle.
- **Mechanical condition:** Resale prospects often depend on whether the vehicle runs and the extent of any engine or transmission problems.
- **Exterior and interior condition:** Accident damage, missing parts, and cabin wear may reduce the vehicle’s value.
- **Vehicle completeness:** The presence of major parts may influence the vehicle’s usefulness for resale or dismantling.
- **Ownership documents:** The paperwork available can affect whether and how the sale can proceed.

To make the estimate more useful, provide accurate details and disclose known problems. If you receive another offer, compare the amount you will actually receive, including towing and other charges, rather than looking at the headline price alone.

## Can You Sell Your Car Without a Title in Texas?

A car without a title is not necessarily a dead end; it depends on the vehicle and the sale. Tell iBuyVehicles about your situation and confirm which documents you need before arranging the pickup.

If the car is titled to you and the title is lost, the recorded owner can request a certified copy of the title with Form VTR-34. Effective January 1, 2026, a specific Texas exemption lets a licensed Used Auto Parts Recycler buy a vehicle without a title if it is at least 13 years old, has not been registered for at least seven years, and is purchased for parts, dismantling, or scrap. Other vehicles generally need acceptable ownership evidence. Check with TxDMV if you are unsure.

For a standard Texas-titled vehicle sale, the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles advises sellers to submit a Vehicle Transfer Notification within 30 days. Keep a record of the sale, including the buyer’s details, date, and VIN, and confirm the paperwork needed for your particular transaction before handing over the vehicle.
