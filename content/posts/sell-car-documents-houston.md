## What Is the Most Important Document to Sell a Car?

The vehicle title is the most critical document. It proves legal ownership. Without it, you cannot transfer ownership to a buyer. In Texas, the title must be in your name. It must also be free of liens. If there is a lien, you need a release letter from your lender.

Texas titles are issued by the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV). They are pink in color. The front shows the vehicle identification number (VIN) and owner information. The back has a transfer section. This is where you sign to sell the car.

## What If You Lost Your Car Title?

Losing your title is stressful. But it is not the end. You can still sell your car. In Texas, the recorded owner can request a certified copy of the title with Form VTR-34, in person at a TxDMV Regional Service Center or by mail. TxDMV says to allow at least 15 days for a mailed copy.

**I Buy Vehicles** in Houston can talk you through it if you lost your title. Texas has a specific no-title route for vehicles at least 13 years old that have not been registered for at least seven years, sold to a licensed Used Auto Parts Recycler. For other cars, registration and ID alone do not replace a title, so a certified copy is usually the first step.

## What Is a Bill of Sale in Texas?

A bill of sale is a legal record of the transaction. It protects both buyer and seller. In Texas, it is not always required for private sales. But it is highly recommended. It proves the car was sold on a certain date. It also records the sale price.

You can find a bill of sale template on the Texas DMV website. It should include:

- The date of sale
- Buyer and seller names and addresses
- The vehicle’s make, model, year, and VIN
- The sale price
- Signatures from both parties

This document is essential for tax purposes. It also helps if there are future disputes.

## What Is the Odometer Disclosure Statement?

Federal law requires an odometer disclosure for most vehicles, though some older vehicles are exempt. Check with TxDMV if you are unsure whether yours is. This form records the vehicle’s exact mileage at the time of sale. It prevents odometer fraud. The seller must sign it. The buyer receives a copy.

The odometer statement is on the back of the Texas title. You fill it out during the transfer. Be accurate. The buyer could check the mileage against service records. Incorrect information can lead to legal trouble.

## What Are Texas DMV Requirements for Car Sales?

The Texas DMV has specific rules for selling a car. You need to complete several steps.

### Complete the Title Transfer

The seller signs the title. The buyer takes the signed title to a county tax office. They have 30 days to transfer it into their name. The seller should remove their license plates. Plates stay with the seller in Texas.

### Submit a Vehicle Transfer Notification

Texas law requires sellers to notify the DMV within 30 days. You can do this online. This step releases you from liability. If the buyer gets a ticket, you will not be responsible.

### Check Emissions Inspection Rules

Texas ended annual safety inspections for most non-commercial passenger vehicles on January 1, 2025. Emissions testing continues in some counties, including Harris County, so a Houston buyer may need a passing emissions test to register the car. Check with TxDMV or the Harris County tax office for current requirements.

### Register the Car

The buyer must register the car in their name. They pay the registration fee and sales tax. The car cannot be driven legally without registration.

## Why Is VIN Verification Important?

The VIN is a unique 17-digit number. It is the car’s fingerprint. It matches the title, registration, and the physical vehicle. The buyer should verify the VIN on the dashboard and door jamb. They should check it against the title.

VIN verification is important for several reasons:

- It proves the car is not stolen.
- It ensures the title matches the car.
- It helps uncover title washing or odometer fraud.
- It is required for out-of-state vehicle registration.

## How Does a Lien Release Work?

A lien release is a document from a lender. It confirms you have paid off your car loan. The lender no longer has a claim on the vehicle. This allows you to transfer the title to a new owner.

If you still owe money, the process is different. You may need to pay off the loan before selling. The buyer could pay the lender directly. In that case, the lender will send the title to the buyer. **I Buy Vehicles** can talk you through how a payoff would work.

## What Vehicle History Report Do Sellers Need?

A vehicle history report is a must-have for modern car sales. Buyers often check the VIN online. They look for flood damage, odometer issues, or salvage titles. This report builds trust with the buyer. It also justifies your asking price.

You can get a report from companies like Carfax or AutoCheck. It shows:

- Previous owners
- Accident history
- Service records
- Title history
- Lien information
- Odometer readings

## Do You Need Proof of Insurance to Sell a Car?

You do not need proof of insurance to sell a car in Texas. However, you should cancel your policy after the sale. Do not cancel before. The car must be insured to be driven legally. You need coverage for test drives. You also need it until the title is signed over.

After the sale, contact your insurance company. Tell them the date of sale. They will remove the car from your policy. Keep a record of your cancellation request.
