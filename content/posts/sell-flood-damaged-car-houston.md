## What Happens During Professional Flood Damage Assessment

Legitimate damaged car buyers follow a clear process when evaluating flooded vehicles. Understanding this helps you recognize fair operators versus scammers.

First, they inspect water line height. Water below the floorboards versus water in the engine bay means completely different valuations. They check for sediment in fluids, corrosion on electrical connectors, and mold in the HVAC system. These factors determine whether the car becomes parts donor or scrap metal.

Next comes vehicle appraisal based on salvage category. Texas issues salvage titles for repairable flood damage and non-repairable certificates for severe cases. Buyers price accordingly, knowing they cannot legally return some vehicles to road use.

Finally, they factor tow pickup service logistics into the offer. If your car sits in a flooded garage or stranded parking lot, free towing adds real value to the deal.
